## Acting officers need command reps before the call

Acting officers often step into leadership with limited chances to practice the full command role. They may know the tactics, but still need repetitions giving reports, assigning companies, and maintaining control of a developing incident.

3AlarmLabs helps acting officers practice those reps in a focused, repeatable way.

## What acting officers can practice

- First-arriving size-up
- Establishing command
- Clear radio traffic
- Assigning incoming companies
- Tracking progress as conditions change

## Better preparation for real responsibility

Acting officer training should give firefighters a chance to make decisions before those decisions affect crews on an emergency scene. Scenario practice helps build confidence, timing, and command presence.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is this useful for new acting officers?

Yes. New acting officers can use self-paced scenarios to practice the flow of command before serving in the role.

### Can a training officer review the run?

Yes. Replay review gives instructors a way to coach radio communication, assignments, and decision-making.

### Does this work for career and volunteer departments?

Yes. Acting officer development matters in career, volunteer, and combination departments.
