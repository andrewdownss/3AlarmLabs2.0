## Practice the command skills promotion processes care about

Promotion is not only about knowing policy or memorizing tactics. Firefighters preparing for officer roles need to show that they can size up conditions, communicate clearly, assign resources, and stay organized.

3AlarmLabs gives candidates a way to practice those skills repeatedly through self-paced command scenarios.

## Skills to build before the test

- Clear initial radio reports
- Tactical priorities and decision-making
- Company assignments and accountability
- Command board organization
- After-action review and self-correction

## Why self-paced reps matter

Promotional prep often happens around shifts, family time, and department schedules. Self-paced command training lets firefighters practice more often without waiting for a classroom session or formal drill.

3AlarmLabs can help candidates practice command thinking, communication, and decision flow that are often important in promotional processes.

Yes. Departments can use 3AlarmLabs to support acting officers, company officers, and promotional prep programs.
