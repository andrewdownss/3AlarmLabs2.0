## Make every command rep easier to review

After-action review is where command training turns into improvement. Firefighters need to see what happened, hear what was said, and talk through decisions while the run is still fresh.

3AlarmLabs supports replay review so each scenario can become a focused coaching session.

## What to review after a scenario

- Initial size-up and priorities
- Radio communication clarity
- Company assignments
- Timing of decisions
- Command board organization

## Why replay matters

Memory alone is not enough. Replay gives firefighters and instructors a shared record of the scenario, which makes feedback more specific and useful.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is after-action review in fire training?

After-action review is a structured conversation after training that looks at what happened, why it happened, and what should improve next time.

### Can 3AlarmLabs review radio traffic?

Yes. Radio traffic and transcripts can be part of the review process.

### Is review useful for self-paced training?

Yes. Self-paced firefighters can use replay review to compare runs and focus on improvement.
