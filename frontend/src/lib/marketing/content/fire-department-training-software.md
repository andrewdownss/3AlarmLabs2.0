## Fire department training software for command development

Fire departments need training that is practical, repeatable, and easy to run. Live drills matter, but departments also need ways to build decision-making reps between scheduled evolutions.

3AlarmLabs helps departments train command skills with scenario-based practice, radio communication, command board tracking, and after-action review.

## Where it fits in a department program

- Self-paced command practice for members
- Instructor-led command sessions for groups
- Officer development and promotional prep
- Radio communication coaching
- Replay review after each scenario

## Why departments use software

Software makes it easier to standardize training across shifts, give members more repetitions, and preserve a record of what happened during a scenario. Training officers can focus coaching on decisions, communication, and tactical priorities instead of rebuilding every drill from scratch.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does this replace live training?

No. 3AlarmLabs is designed to add more command reps between live drills, tabletop exercises, and field evolutions.

### Can every member train?

Yes. Department plans are built for member access so firefighters can practice self-paced scenarios in addition to instructor-led sessions.

### Can instructors review performance?

Yes. Replay review helps instructors coach radio communication, assignments, timing, and command organization.
