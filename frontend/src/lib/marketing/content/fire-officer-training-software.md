## Software for fire officer development

Fire officers need more than classroom information. They need repeated practice making decisions, communicating assignments, and organizing resources while an incident develops.

3AlarmLabs gives officers and future officers a practical way to build those reps through scenario-based command training.

## What officers practice

- Reading conditions and setting priorities
- Initial reports and follow-up radio traffic
- Assigning companies and tracking actions
- Managing incident flow
- Reviewing decisions after the run

## Built for ongoing development

Officer development should not happen only before a test or once a year at a drill. With self-paced and instructor-led options, departments can make command practice part of regular training.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is this for?

3AlarmLabs is useful for acting officers, company officers, firefighters preparing for promotion, training officers, and departments building officer development programs.

### Can firefighters train without an instructor?

Yes. Self-paced scenarios let individual firefighters practice command on their own schedule.

### Can departments run instructor-led sessions?

Yes. Department plans support instructor-led Command sessions and shared training content.
